Our game plan

Cozy daily life with a serious fantasy story. Connected towns and explorable regions.

Choose class, gender, hairstyle, appearance and outfit. Planned classes: warrior, archer, mage, rogue. Later: real-time combat, dodging, blocking, special abilities, goblins and dragons.

Build and furnish homes, discover and adopt cats and dogs. Shared multiplayer world and progression is the goal. Death: return home and lose some money.

This prototype: one village, one house, character creation and local saves. Combat and multiplayer are not implemented. No homepage icon until the game is finished.
