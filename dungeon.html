How to play

Choose an enemy, then choose an action. Basic attacks recover Focus. Spend Focus on skills and finishers. Enemies reveal their next response, so you can guard, break their shield, or use a potion before a dangerous hit.

Every action builds the shared combo meter. At six points, United Strike hits every enemy. Protect, heal, and revive use the ally you select. Camp and relic choices benefit the entire party. You never need to wait for another player’s turn.
